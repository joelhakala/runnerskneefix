## 101-no-holy-grail  (M9)

Tried everything and nothing was THE fix? Maybe there was never one cause.

A long-run jump, a few weeks off, an old sprain, tight quads, new shoes. None of them overflows the cup alone. Together they do. That is also why no single fix removes it, and why two or three small changes often do.

This is a model, not a diagnosis. What is in your cup?

Education only, not medical advice.
Source: Peterson 2022

## 102-rest-trap  (M4)

Rested for weeks and the pain came back on the first run?

Rest quiets the pain. It may also lower what your knee is ready for, so the same old run is suddenly too much. An alternative to zero: drop just under the line, then build.

This loop is a hypothesis built on load-management research, not a proven mechanism.

Education only, not medical advice.
Source: Esculier 2018

## 103-mismatch  (M1)

Weak glutes. Bad tracking. Flat feet. Wrong shoes.

Studies keep hunting for the one flaw, and it rarely predicts who gets hurt. What shows up more often: load jumped past what the knee was ready for. And ready is trainable, step by step.

Education only, not medical advice.
Source: Neal 2019

## 104-same-scan  (M2)

In one study, 97% of knees in adults WITHOUT symptoms showed at least one finding on MRI.

Same picture, one knee hurts, one doesn't. Pain works more like an alarm than a damage report.

Sharp pain, swelling, locking or giving way are different: get those checked.

Education only, not medical advice.
Source: Horga 2020

## 105-not-tyres  (M3)

"Running will wear out your knees." True for tyres.

Living tissue adapts to load. In a review of 114,829 people, hip and knee arthritis was found in 3.5% of recreational runners and 10.2% of non-runners. Elite level: 13.3%.

Association, not proof.

Education only, not medical advice.
Source: Alentorn-Geli 2017

## 106-three-groups  (M5)

Hip exercises or knee exercises?

A trial put 112 people into three groups: hips, knees, or just staying active. After one year: no difference. All three got the same coaching on how much to load.

Exercise still helps. The type mattered less than the dose.

Education only, not medical advice.
Source: Hott 2019

Where exactly does it hurt?

Front, outside, below the kneecap, inside. Different tissues, same rule: match the load to what the tissue is ready for.

## 108-load-lands  (M7)

Why the knee and not the ankle?

How you run sets how load is shared. Heel-first with a long stride sends more to the knee. Forefoot with quick steps sends less to the knee and more to the Achilles.

Load doesn't vanish, it moves. If you change technique, change it slowly.

Education only, not medical advice.
Source: Kulmala 2013

## 109-massage  (M8)

Does massage fix runner's knee?

In a forum sample we coded, 7 runners said it saved them and 7 said it did nothing. Look closer: 5 of the 7 were also doing something else.

Massage seems to turn the alarm down for a while. That's a window. Use it to load.

Forum reports, not a study.

Education only, not medical advice.
Source: Forum sample

## 110-longest-run  (M1)

Counting weekly miles?

In a study of 5,200 runners and 588,000 runs, injury rate tracked one thing: how far a single run went past your longest run of the last 30 days. 10-30% past it: 64% higher. Double: 128% higher.

Before your next long run, check your longest in the last 30 days.

Education only, not medical advice.
Source: Frandsen 2025

## 111-stretching  (M8)

Does stretching make a muscle longer?

After weeks of stretching you reach further. But the muscle is barely longer: what moved is the point where your alarm goes off.

That's why it feels good, and why tightness can come back. Pair it with loading.

Education only, not medical advice.
Source: Weppler & Magnusson 2010

## 112-arthritis  (M3)

Told to stop running because of knee arthritis?

A 4-year study followed 1,203 people over 50 with knee arthritis. About 1 in 9 ran. Their knees did not get worse faster, and their pain eased more often.

They chose to run, so this is not proof. Ask your own clinician.

Education only, not medical advice.
Source: Lo 2018
